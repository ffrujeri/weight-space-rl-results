Can Astra weight updates replace PPO?

Direct comparison from random initialization. Completed.

Astra repeatedly collects experience and assigns new neural-policy parameters. PPO repeatedly collects experience and applies its normal learning update. No PPO or subsequent RL fine-tuning improves the Astra policies.

All six training runs and all 27 registered policy evaluations are complete. Results below include every seed and checkpoint.

Astra

Collect 49,152 interactions → inspect experience → edit policy weights → repeat.

32 rounds per independent editor. All MLP weights and exploration std are editable. No policy gradients or numerical policy optimizer.

PPO

Collect rollouts → PPO updates → repeat.

Native PPO hyperparameters, 64 environments and 24 steps per update. 32 PPO iterations match one Astra feedback block.

Results: Astra updates versus PPO updates

Astra finished with 11.11 higher mean return than PPO and exceeded PPO in 3 of the three pairs. The paired Astra-minus-PPO difference is +11.11 ± 2.82 return units (mean ± sample SD over three pairs).

These are the registered final policies at 1,572,864 interactions each. No best-checkpoint selection or PPO fine-tuning is applied to Astra. The outcome concerns this budget and this Microduck configuration; it is not a general replacement claim.

Each policy score averages 160 first episodes: 32 for each of five fixed commands with neutral head/body commands. This test distribution differs from the training command distribution. Thin curves show individual pairs; uncertainty bars show training-pair SD, not episode-level confidence intervals.

Registered comparison

  • Three independent pairs: actor seeds 41, 42 and 43, with a fresh Astra context for each. Both methods start from the exact same random actor tensors within each pair.
  • Same interaction budget: 1,572,864 training interactions per arm per pair; 9,437,184 total across six arms. All Astra feedback experience is charged.
  • Same task and preprocessing: pinned Microduck stationary configuration, rewards and architecture; native online actor observation normalization in both arms. Both start with Gaussian std 1.0 and control it through their learning method.
  • Primary outcome: held-out final episode return, with paired learning curves at 0, 4, 8, 16 and 32 feedback-block budgets. Survival and command response explain the behavior behind the return.
  • No selection from tests: freeze every checkpoint before testing all 27 unique policies for 4,320 first episodes. Identical initial policies are evaluated once per pair. Astra receives no test scores or PPO results.

The failed source/log and same-seed correction are preserved. No experimental editor used those smoke trajectories.

Three pairs provide a first replicated test, not a broad claim across tasks or converged budgets. Initialization, environment and editor randomness vary jointly. PPO retains its native privileged critic observations; Astra receives actor trajectories and task semantics. Matching interactions does not match prior knowledge, inference compute or cost. End-to-end and collection/waiting times are recorded separately; model token/billing telemetry may be unavailable.
